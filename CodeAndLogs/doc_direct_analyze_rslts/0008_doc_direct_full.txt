Android support — No. Apple CloudKit is Apple’s iCloud-backed framework/service intended for Apple platforms, with official client frameworks for iOS, macOS, etc. It is not offered as an official Android SDK/library, nor is Android a documented native integration target for CloudKit SDK. Apple does provide web/server access patterns for CloudKit in some contexts, but that is not an official Android SDK integration.
