SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support  
Adobe Primetime TVSDK had an official Android SDK/integration path for building Android video playback apps. Adobe documentation and release materials historically referenced TVSDK for Android alongside other platforms. Step 1 is therefore yes.

2. Service model  
Primetime TVSDK is primarily a client-side video playback SDK. Its core role is playback, ad handling, and media/DRM integration inside the app. While it can interact with remote endpoints during playback (media manifests, ad servers, DRM/license servers), those are not inherently Adobe-hosted TVSDK backend services that the SDK itself must be registered against for normal operation.

3. Integration requirement  
For normal Android integration of TVSDK itself, there is no clear evidence that the developer must obtain a TVSDK-specific console-issued API key, App ID, client secret, or equivalent from Adobe and embed it in the app. Typical setup appears to involve adding the SDK and configuring playback/ad/DRM endpoints, not registering the app for a vendor-issued credential tied to TVSDK.

4. Scope  
Some related Adobe Primetime components or adjacent services may involve credentials or configuration values, and third-party DRM/ad systems can also require their own identifiers. Those do not by themselves make TVSDK an SDK with a required Adobe-issued API credential. Based on the product-specific integration pattern, the credential requirement for TVSDK itself is not established. Evidence is somewhat dated, so I prefer false/null.
