SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. **Android support** - Yes. Kakao SDK has official Android integration for Kakao platform features such as login, sharing, and related social/platform services.

2. **Service model** - This SDK is not primarily an on-device utility library. Its standard use is to connect the app to Kakao-hosted platform services such as authentication and Kakao social/share features.

3. **Integration requirement** - Yes. Typical Kakao SDK setup requires the developer to register the application in Kakao Developers and configure a console-issued application identifier in the Android app. The standard credential used for SDK integration is the **Native App Key**.

4. **Scope** - This credential belongs to Kakao SDK’s own documented integration, not merely to some separate Kakao product. Android package name and signing settings may also be part of app registration, but those alone are not the credential; the relevant console-issued value is the app key used by the SDK.
