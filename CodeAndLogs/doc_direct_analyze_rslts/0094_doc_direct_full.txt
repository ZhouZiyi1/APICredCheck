SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support — No.
Masonry is a well-known Auto Layout DSL for Apple platforms, maintained under the SnapKit organization, and is used for iOS/macOS layout constraints in Objective-C. It is not an Android SDK and does not have an official Android library or Android integration documentation for standard Android app use.
