SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. **Android support** - Yes. Huawei Location Kit has official Android integration as part of Huawei Mobile Services for Android apps.

2. **Service model** - It is not purely on-device. Location Kit provides location-related capabilities through HMS APIs and commonly works with Huawei’s service framework/backend-enabled platform features, rather than being just a local utility library.

3. **Integration requirement** - Yes. Standard Huawei SDK integration typically requires registering the app in Huawei’s developer console and adding the Huawei configuration to the Android app. For HMS kits including Location Kit, the normal setup uses the app’s Huawei project/app registration and shipped config to identify the app to Huawei services.

4. **Scope** - This credentialing/config belongs to the documented integration of Huawei Location Kit on Android, not merely to some unrelated Huawei product. The relevant integration artifact is Huawei’s app configuration, whose standard purpose is to provide identifiers for HMS service access.
