SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support  
Yes. The Google Play License Verification Library (LVL) is an Android-specific library provided for apps distributed through Google Play to verify licensing status on Android devices.

2. Service model  
This product is not purely on-device. In normal use, the app uses LVL together with Google Play’s licensing mechanism, which depends on Google Play services / Play infrastructure to check whether the user is licensed for the app. The library validates signed licensing responses rather than operating as a standalone local utility.

3. Integration requirement  
Yes. Standard LVL integration requires the developer to obtain the app’s licensing public key from the Google Play Console and include/configure it in the Android app so signed license responses can be verified.

4. Scope  
That key is part of this product’s documented integration flow for Google Play licensing, not just a separate Google product added independently. Package name or signing alone would not satisfy the requirement; the Play Console-issued licensing key is the relevant configured value.
