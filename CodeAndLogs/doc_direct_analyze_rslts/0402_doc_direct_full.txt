SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support: Yes. TikTok Business SDK has official Android integration documentation and an Android library for adding app-event measurement / attribution in Android apps.

2. Service model: This SDK is not mainly on-device. Its normal purpose is to send install, event, and attribution-related data from the app to TikTok for Business backend services for measurement, optimization, and ads reporting.

3. Integration requirement: Yes. Standard setup requires the developer to register/configure the app in TikTok for Business and add console-issued app credentials to the Android integration. The documented setup commonly uses an `App ID` together with a token value (`Client Token` / similar access credential) so the SDK can authenticate traffic to TikTok-hosted services.

4. Scope: These credentials are part of the TikTok Business SDK integration itself, not merely package-name or signing metadata, and not only for some separate TikTok product. They are used so this SDK can communicate with TikTok Business services.
