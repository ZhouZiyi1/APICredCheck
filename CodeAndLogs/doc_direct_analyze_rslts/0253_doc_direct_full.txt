SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support  
Huawei Ads Kit has official Android support as part of HMS Core, with documented Android integration through the Huawei Ads SDK.

2. Service model  
This SDK routinely depends on Huawei-hosted ad delivery services. Its standard purpose is to request, load, and display ads from Huawei’s backend rather than operate purely on-device.

3. Integration requirement  
Normal Android integration requires the developer to register the app in Huawei’s developer/AppGallery Connect ecosystem and configure vendor-issued identifiers in the app. Huawei Ads Kit setup commonly includes the Huawei `App ID`, and actual ad serving also uses Ads Kit ad slot identifiers created in Huawei’s console.

4. Scope  
The credential is part of this product’s documented integration, not merely another optional Huawei service. Ads Kit’s app/ad-slot identification is used so Huawei’s ad service can recognize the calling app and serve inventory.
