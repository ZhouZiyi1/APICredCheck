SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support: No. Apple's iMessage SDK refers to the Messages/iMessage app extension APIs used to build iMessage apps and sticker packs within Apple's Messages ecosystem on Apple platforms. Apple documents these APIs for iOS/iPadOS (and related Apple platform contexts), not as an Android SDK or Android integration. There is no official Android library or documented Android setup for integrating iMessage SDK features into an Android app.

Because step 1 is no, steps 2-4 are skipped as not applicable on Android.
