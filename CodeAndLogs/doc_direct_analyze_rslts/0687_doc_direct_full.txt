SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support: Yes. Volc VOD has official Android integration in ByteDance/Volcengine documentation, including Android player/upload SDK setup.

2. Service model: This is not just an on-device utility library. VOD is built around ByteDance/Volcengine hosted video services such as media storage, processing, playback authorization, and related cloud delivery workflows. The Android SDK is typically a client for that hosted VOD platform.

3. Integration requirement: Yes. Normal Android integration commonly requires a vendor-issued SDK/player license configured in the app for the official VOD/player SDK, and playback flows also depend on vendor-side authorization material. The clearest app-configured console-issued credential for the Android SDK itself is the player license.

4. Scope: This credential is part of the documented Volc VOD Android integration itself, not merely a separate unrelated ByteDance product. While some request tokens may be generated by the developer's backend, the SDK/license requirement belongs to the VOD SDK integration.
