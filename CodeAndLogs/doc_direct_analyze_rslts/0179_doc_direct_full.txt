SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support  
Yes. Umeng provides an official Android Analytics SDK/integration for mobile apps, so this product supports Android.

2. Service model  
uMeng Analytics is not a purely on-device library. Its standard purpose is to collect app usage/event data and send it to Umeng's hosted analytics backend for aggregation, reporting, and dashboards.

3. Integration requirement  
Yes. Standard Android integration for Umeng Analytics requires the developer to register the app in Umeng's console and configure an issued app identifier in the Android app. In Umeng documentation/integration materials, this is typically the `Appkey` (often alongside a manually chosen channel value, but the channel is not a vendor-issued credential).

4. Scope  
This credential is part of the Analytics SDK's own setup, not just another Umeng product. The required `Appkey` identifies the app to Umeng Analytics services so collected data can be attributed to the correct project.
