SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support: No. ARKit is Apple's native augmented reality framework for Apple platforms such as iOS, iPadOS, and related Apple OS environments. Apple does not provide an official Android ARKit SDK or documented Android integration for normal app development. On Android, the comparable platform product is typically ARCore from Google, not ARKit.
