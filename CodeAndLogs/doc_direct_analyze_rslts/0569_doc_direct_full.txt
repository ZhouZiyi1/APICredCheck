Step 1: Android support

`Harpy SDK` by Arthur Ariel Sabintsev is documented and known as an iOS/macOS/tvOS/watchOS Swift library for checking the Apple App Store for newer app versions and prompting users to update. Its standard integration targets Apple platforms and Apple distribution metadata. I found no official Android library or Android integration documentation for this product.

Because step 1 is no, steps 2-4 are skipped. They are not applicable on Android for this SDK.
