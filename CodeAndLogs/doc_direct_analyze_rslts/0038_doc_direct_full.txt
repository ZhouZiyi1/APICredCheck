SECTION 1: Detailed Analysis

1. Android support — No. SDWebImage is an image downloading/caching library primarily for Apple platforms (iOS, macOS, tvOS, watchOS, visionOS) maintained as an Objective-C/Swift ecosystem project. Its standard documentation and package distribution are for Apple platforms, not Android. There is no official Android SDK/integration for SDWebImage itself.
