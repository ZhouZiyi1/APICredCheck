**2. Likely Protection Signals**

- `Package name`
  - `smali/com/google/android/libraries/places/internal/zzjc.smali`
  - `smali/com/google/android/libraries/places/internal/zznh.smali`
  - Both files contain `X-Android-Package` and `Context->getPackageName()`, which strongly indicates package-name binding in outbound Places requests.
- `Signing certificate digest`
  - `smali/com/google/android/libraries/places/internal/zzmr.smali`
  - Reads `PackageInfo.signatures[]`, uses `MessageDigest.getInstance("SHA-1")`, and digests the certificate bytes.
  - `smali/com/google/android/libraries/places/internal/zzjc.smali` and `zznh.smali` contain `X-Android-Cert`, which strongly suggests `zzmr` feeds that header.
- `Manifest metadata`
  - `AndroidManifest.xml`: `com.google.android.geo.API_KEY`
  - This is likely the root credential source for the SDK.
- `Network fields used by SDK servers`
  - `X-Goog-Api-Key` in `zzfh.smali` and `CctTransportBackend.smali`
  - `X-Android-Package` in `zzjc.smali` and `zznh.smali`
  - `X-Android-Cert` in `zzjc.smali` and `zznh.smali`
